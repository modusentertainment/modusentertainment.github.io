What is Kakuro? It is the sudoku variant also known as Cross Sums. Every run of white squares has to add up to the number at its head, and no digit repeats inside a run. There is no arithmetic drilling in it — it is pure logic, and it never needs a guess.

If you get on with these, the full book has 120 of them, each with its own illustration, running from cosy farmland through tropical islands to snowy mountain forests.
