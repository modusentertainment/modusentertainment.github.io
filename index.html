Kakuro 2-in-1 Puzzle Book (+Coloring)

120 Grids, Beginner Volume — The Next-Level Sudoku Challenge
Paperback, 8.5 × 11 in · 134 pages · by Modus Racio

Kakuro is a number puzzle solved by logic rather than arithmetic — if you can solve a Sudoku, you can learn it in a few minutes. This book pairs it with something no other puzzle book offers: behind every one of the 120 grids sits an original full-page illustration, drawn in black and white so it can be coloured in. Solve it, colour it, or both.

Every grid was generated by our own software and individually checked to have exactly one valid solution. None of them appear in any other book. Full solutions are included.
